# 1. PROJECT OVERVIEW

## 1.1 Project Objective

Develop a complete, modern, secure, responsive and scalable Hospital Management System (HMS) for a multispecialty hospital.

The system must combine:

1. Public Hospital Website
2. Hospital Administration
3. Patient Management
4. Doctor Management
5. Appointment Management
6. OPD Management
7. IPD Management
8. Laboratory Management
9. Radiology Management
10. Pharmacy Management
11. Billing & Accounting
12. Bed & Ward Management
13. Operation Theatre Management
14. Nursing Management
15. Emergency Management
16. HR & Payroll
17. Inventory & Procurement
18. Reports & Analytics
19. Patient Portal
20. System Administration & Security

The system must support complete hospital workflows, interconnected modules, real-time operational updates where technically supported, and centralized management.

## 1.2 Core Goals

- Digitize hospital operations.
- Reduce manual paperwork.
- Manage patient records centrally.
- Improve doctor and patient scheduling.
- Track laboratory and radiology reports.
- Manage medicine inventory.
- Automate billing and payments.
- Monitor hospital resources.
- Improve financial transparency.
- Provide secure patient access.
- Generate operational and financial reports.

# 36. BUSINESS RULES

1. Every patient must have a unique UHID.
2. Every appointment must have a unique appointment reference.
3. Every admission must have a unique admission number.
4. Every invoice must have a unique invoice number.
5. Bed allocation must check current availability.
6. Appointment booking must check doctor availability.
7. Pharmacy sales must validate available stock.
8. Medicine expiry must be checked before dispensing.
9. Lab reports must follow defined verification and approval workflows.
10. Financial transactions must be auditable.
11. Refunds must follow authorized approval.
12. Only authorized users can view sensitive medical records.
13. Clinical records must maintain revision history.
14. User actions must be recorded in audit logs.
15. Duplicate patient detection must be supported.
16. Every module must validate its input.
17. Critical alerts must be visible to authorized personnel.
18. Discharged patients must not retain active bed allocation.
19. Cancelled transactions must not silently modify financial history.
20. Branch-specific data must be isolated according to permissions.
